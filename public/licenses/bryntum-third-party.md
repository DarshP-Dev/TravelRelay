# Third Party Notices

Bryntum uses the following third party libraries:

* [Font Awesome 6 Free](https://fontawesome.com/license/free) (MIT/SIL OFL 1.1)
* [Poppins font (only used in our demos)](https://github.com/itfoundry/Poppins) (SIL OFL 1.1)
* [Roboto Flex font (only used in our demos, for the Fluent 2 and Material 3 themes)](https://github.com/googlefonts/roboto-flex) (SIL OFL 1.1)
* [Montserrat font (only used in our demos, for the Svalbard theme)](https://github.com/JulietaUla/Montserrat) (SIL OFL 1.1)
* [Tree Walker polyfill (only applies to Salesforce)](https://github.com/Krinkle/dom-TreeWalker-polyfill) (MIT)
* [chronograph](https://github.com/bryntum/chronograph) (MIT)
* [Monaco editor (only used in our demos)](https://microsoft.github.io/monaco-editor) (MIT)
* Map/Set polyfill to fix performance issues for Salesforce LWS (MIT)
* [AG Charts Community (when using Chart package)](https://github.com/ag-grid/ag-charts) (MIT)
* [AG Charts Enterprise (optional, when using Chart package)](https://www.ag-grid.com/charts/) (Commercial)

Note: the **chronograph** library is used by the scheduling engine of Bryntum Scheduler, Gantt, Calendar and TaskBoard.
TreeWalker is only used in the LWC bundle for Salesforce. The themes use no fonts of their own, the fonts are only
loaded by our demos.
